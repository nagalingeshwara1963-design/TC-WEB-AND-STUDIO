# TC Web & Studio: Project Brief

## Business Overview
TC Web & Studio is a founder-led creative and digital studio based in Mysuru, Karnataka, India. The studio serves clients locally and remotely, bringing brand strategy, content, film, photography, and website development together under one roof. The founders—Naveen, Nidhith, and Hemanth—work collaboratively to deliver tailored projects across various industries.

## Objectives
- Establish the official portfolio and lead-generation website for TC Web & Studio.
- Create a serious, long-term business asset prioritizing quality, originality, maintainability, performance, and conversion.
- Showcase a curated portfolio that highlights complementary creative and technical skills.
- Facilitate business inquiries and lead generation from local and remote clients.

## Audiences
Potential clients across various sectors, including but not limited to:
- Educational institutions and coaching academies
- Automotive dealerships and businesses
- Restaurants and hospitality businesses
- Farms and agriculture-related businesses
- Real estate businesses
- Medical practices and doctors
- Personal brands
- Startups and established companies

**Geographic Focus:** 
Primary market is Mysuru, Karnataka, with operations extending across India and potential for future international clients.

## Scope
**In-Scope:**
- Design and development of a responsive, high-performance static-first website.
- Categorized portfolio presentation with individual case study pages.
- Lead-generation features (contact forms, WhatsApp integration).
- Multi-language support structure (English primary; Kannada, Hindi planned).
- Foundational SEO and analytics setup.
- Basic blog/insights section architecture.

**Out-of-Scope (for initial launch):**
- Paid digital marketing services for the website itself.
- Fully functional visual CMS (file-based editing will be used initially).
- Client portal/dashboard (to be documented as a potential future phase).

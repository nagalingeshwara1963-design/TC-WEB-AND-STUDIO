# TC Web & Studio: Design System

## Visual Direction
**Theme:** Light, clean, editorial, and premium.
The aesthetic should reflect a considered, independent creative studio rather than a generic corporate agency. The design will prioritize content, utilizing negative space and strong typography to let the portfolio work stand out.

## Typography Recommendations
- **Style:** Strong editorial typography with a deliberate, high-contrast type scale.
- **Headings:** A refined serif or a distinctive, sharp sans-serif (e.g., Playfair Display, Instrument Serif, or Inter/Switzer).
- **Body:** Highly readable, clean sans-serif.
- *(Final selection pending brand guidelines confirmation.)*

## Colour Strategy
- **Backgrounds:** Warm white or off-white surfaces to reduce eye strain and feel premium.
- **Text/Borders:** Near-black or deep charcoal for maximum readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Accent Colour:** ONE restrained accent colour, derived from the official brand assets once approved. Used sparingly for interaction states and key highlights.

## Layout and Spacing Principles
- **Whitespace:** Generous and deliberate to frame content and imagery.
- **Grids:** Asymmetric, editorial grids, particularly for the portfolio, mixing image aspect ratios (e.g., 3:4, 16:9) to create visual interest.
- **Hierarchy:** Clear distinction between sections using scale and spacing rather than heavy boxes or borders.

## Component Principles
- **Simplicity:** Components (buttons, cards, inputs) should be minimal, avoiding excessive shadows or gradients.
- **Interactions:** Refined hover states, subtle transitions, and clear focus states for accessibility.
- **Imagery:** Large, art-directed imagery that acts as the focal point of the page.

## Animation Principles
- Subtle scroll motion to reveal content gently.
- CSS transitions for interactions (hovers, form inputs).
- Avoid gratuitous animations; every effect must support storytelling or navigation.

## Responsive Requirements
- Strong mobile-first design, ensuring editorial layouts degrade gracefully to single-column on small screens.
- Touch-friendly navigation and tap targets (minimum 44x44px).

## Accessibility Requirements
- High colour contrast for all text (WCAG AA minimum).
- Semantic HTML tags (`<nav>`, `<main>`, `<article>`).
- Full keyboard navigability with visible focus rings.
- ARIA labels where native HTML falls short.

## Inspiration (SiteInspire Lessons)
- Treat the portfolio like an art gallery: the website is the frame, the work is the art.
- Use typography as a primary design element.
- Embrace asymmetry to avoid the "template" look.

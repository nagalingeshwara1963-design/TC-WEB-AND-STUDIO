# TC Web & Studio: Sitemap & User Flows

## Proposed Sitemap
- **Home:** Introduction, core positioning, featured work, service divisions overview, call to action.
- **About / Studio:** The founder-led approach, team introductions, methodology, values.
- **Work (Portfolio):** Filterable gallery of case studies.
  - **Project Detail Pages (Dynamic):** Deep dives into individual projects, showing deliverables and outcomes.
- **Services:**
  - Brand & Design
  - Social & Growth
  - Websites & Search
  - Films & Photography (Commercial focus)
- **Pre-Wedding (Dedicated subpage):** Isolated from B2B services, tailored for couples.
- **Insights / Blog:** Articles on strategy, design, and digital trends.
- **Contact:** Enquiry form, direct contact details, WhatsApp integration, location.
- **Legal:** Privacy Policy, Terms of Service.

## Navigation Hierarchy
- **Primary Navigation (Header):** Work, Services, Studio, Insights, Contact (Button). Language Selector.
- **Secondary Navigation (Footer):** Service links, Legal pages, Social media links, Pre-Wedding subpage link.

## Primary and Secondary Calls to Action
- **Primary CTAs:** "Start a Project", "Discuss Your Project" (routes to Contact/Form).
- **Secondary CTAs:** "Explore Our Work", "View All Services".

## Customer Journeys
### Business Services (B2B)
1. **Entry:** Lands on Homepage or specific Service page via SEO/social.
2. **Discovery:** Reads positioning statement, understands the studio's breadth of services.
3. **Validation:** Views relevant Portfolio case studies, confirming quality and relevance.
4. **Action:** Clicks "Start a Project" to fill out the enquiry form or initiate a WhatsApp chat.

### Pre-Wedding Work (B2C)
1. **Entry:** Lands directly on the Pre-Wedding subpage via Instagram/referral link.
2. **Discovery:** Views highly visual, emotional content and video reels isolated from corporate branding services.
3. **Action:** Specific CTA for couples (e.g., "Check Availability" or direct WhatsApp link).

## Lead-Generation Flows
- **Enquiry Form:** Collects Name, Email, Phone, Company, Budget Range, Services required, and project details. Routes via email notification.
- **WhatsApp Contact:** Floating action button or direct link for rapid communication.
- **Appointment Booking:** Potential integration with Cal.com or Calendly for scheduling discovery calls directly from the Contact page.

# TC Web & Studio: Brand Positioning

## Brand Positioning
**Initial Direction:** "An independent creative and digital studio bringing brand strategy, content, film, photography and website development together under one roof."

TC Web & Studio positions itself as a comprehensive, founder-led partner rather than a fragmented freelancer network or a generic corporate agency. The emphasis is on a connected workflow from planning to production and digital execution.

## Differentiators
- **Direct Founder Involvement:** Clients work directly with the experts executing the work.
- **Integrated Skillsets:** Complementary creative and technical skills housed within one team.
- **Locally Rooted, Nationally Capable:** "Mysuru-based. Working across India."
- **Practical Customisation:** Customised project scopes designed around client budget and reality.

## Service Divisions
The studio's offerings are organized into four clear, customer-facing divisions:
1. **Brand & Design:** Brand strategy, identity, branding, graphic/poster design, creative direction, campaign creatives.
2. **Social & Growth:** Social media management/marketing, paid advertising, influencer marketing, lead generation, content strategy.
3. **Websites & Search:** Website design/development, landing pages, digital experiences, SEO, website improvements.
4. **Films & Photography:** Reels, short-form video, commercial video production, photography, video editing, motion graphics.
   *Note: Pre-wedding shoots and films are offered but require a dedicated subpage/journey to avoid confusing business clients.*

## Tone of Voice
Professional, clear, truthful, and quietly confident. 
- Avoid generic marketing jargon and exaggerated claims.
- Focus on craftsmanship, strategy, and measurable impact (when proven).
- The tone should reflect a premium independent studio.

## Claims We Can and Cannot Make
**We Can Claim:**
- Direct collaboration with founders.
- Expertise across design, web, and media production.
- Customised project scopes and pricing.
- Local context for Mysuru, alongside remote capabilities.

**We Cannot Claim (Unless Proven):**
- "Guaranteed" business growth, traffic, or ROI.
- Exclusive specialization in a single industry.
- Specific metrics, awards, or follower growth figures not backed by data.
- Invented years of experience or fabricated client quotes.

- **Typography:** Brand fonts, if already established.
- **Photography:** High-quality team portraits and behind-the-scenes studio imagery.
